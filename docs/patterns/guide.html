Pattern Guide

How to read a candle

Each candle is one period of time (an hour, a day, or a week). Green = price ended higher than it started. Red = it ended lower. The thick part (body) is from where it started to where it ended. The thin lines (wicks) show the highest and lowest prices during that time.

How to read the scores

Grade = how well this pattern actually worked on your 11 stocks over many years. A works well and consistently. B decent. C barely. F loses money. low-n not enough examples yet.

Win % = how often price reached the goal before hitting the stop. R = average profit per trade measured in "risk units": +0.30R means if you risk $100 each time, you average about +$30.
